# Welcome to De Stijl Technology 🚀

We are **De Stijl Technology**, a software company focused on creating innovative solutions for the global market. With a deep commitment to quality and originality, all of our in-game assets and software products are designed 100% in-house. We aim to revolutionize the digital space through creativity and cutting-edge technology.

## Projects and Products 💻

### 🛍️ [PFun Store](https://pfunstore.com)

Our online prize redeem platform, where users can log in and redeem exciting rewards. The platform integrates with our games to provide a seamless experience for players.

### ⛏️ [DxSxT Network](https://dxsxt.network)

Our Web3 mining platform where users mine virtual rewards through upgradeable robots, mining contracts, wallets, and referrals. Backed by the DST token on Solana with on-chain vesting and a vault.

## Key Technologies We Use 🛠️

- **Languages**: TypeScript, Rust
- **Frameworks & Libraries**: React, React Native (Expo), Next.js, Express, Hono, ts-rest, Anchor (Solana)
- **Cloud & DevOps**: AWS, SST, Terraform, GitHub
- **Database**: DynamoDB (Dynamoose / ElectroDB)
